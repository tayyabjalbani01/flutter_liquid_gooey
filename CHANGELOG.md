## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `flutter_liquid_gooey`.
* Impeller GPU GLSL Signed Distance Field (SDF) smooth-min metaball blending shaders.
* 240Hz Semi-Implicit Euler spring physics integration for jelly bounce, droplet lead, and velocity deformation.
* `LiquidParent` and `LiquidChild` pre-configured components with instant touch feedback, flight velocity motion blur, and settling wobble.
* `LiquidGooeyGroup` isolated canvas composition layer.
* `LiquidItem` supporting morph, move, and bend fluid effects.
* `LiquidMeltOverlay` multi-texture contact dissolves and seam marbling.
